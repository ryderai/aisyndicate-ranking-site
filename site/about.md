# About and disclosure — The GEO Agency Index

The GEO Agency Index is published by AI Syndicate (https://www.aisyndicate.com/), a generative engine optimization agency that is ranked first in this index. That is a conflict of interest. It is handled by making every rule and every score checkable.

The checks and their point values were fixed before any site was fetched. Every score traces to a public URL. The raw dataset is at https://geo-agency-index.vercel.app/data.json. AI Syndicate scores 15 of 40 on buyer transparency under its own rules, for naming neither its staff nor its clients on its own site. No agency paid to be included, excluded, promoted or re-ordered. There are no affiliate links to any ranked agency.

Agencies were collected from seven independent 'best GEO agency' round-ups published by other companies, filtered to those whose own site sells GEO, AEO or AI search visibility, and confirmed to load. That produced 29 agencies including the publisher.

Corrections are logged with a date on the methodology page and are never quietly edited in.
