# Also measured — The GEO Agency Index

Five checks were recorded for all 29 agencies on 14 August 2026 and deliberately left out of the score: Prices published, Team named on their own site, Case studies name the client, Case studies carry a number, You can buy without a sales call.

They are not scored because they measure how open a business is with buyers, not whether it is ready for AI search, which is what this index is about. Mixing them into one number would make the number mean less.

Leaving them out cuts against the publisher. AI Syndicate passes only 15 of 40 here — it names no staff on its own site, names no client in a case study, and publishes no result figure that is not anonymised. Those failures are printed on its own profile page and in the table on this page.

Across the index: 10 of 29 publish a price, 27 of 29 name a person, 26 of 29 name a client. Average 28.1 of 40.
